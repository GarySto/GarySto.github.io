Speaking about Microsoft AI with Tracey Pilon

Yesterday I was given an amazing opportunity by the great Tracey Pilon to join her to talk about Microsoft AI - where it’s going, what’s coming and all the announcements inbetween. To a room a of incredible reps for the biggest global agencies (+ 1 client).

While I wasn’t there to speak about what’s happening behind the scenes I was asked to showcase how I’ve been pushing the boundaries of AI and how it’s possible for everybody to do the same. I was asked to demonstrate Microsoft copilot in use, where it’s saving time and where it’s driving huge benefits for myself personally and both for those who have also joined me in using the tools I’ve created.

And I had a laugh.

When I noticed everybody start leaning in and listening to the words I was saying it was exciting. I never knew so many people would be so interested in what I’ve been tinkering away with on the side of my day-to-day job.

So Big, shout out and thank you to the Microsoft MATS team for having me along and giving me the stage 😄 Adam Lynch

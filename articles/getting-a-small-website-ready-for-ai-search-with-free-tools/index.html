Getting a Small Website Ready for AI Search With Free Tools

6 October 2026 · Gary Stow, performance marketing and AI

When I started garystow.co.uk, I wanted to use everything I had learned in years of digital marketing and test it on a site of my own. I followed the latest news, signed up to everything I could, and set out to learn and use the tools properly rather than just read about them.

The Free Tools I Set Up

Everything I am using is free. On my own site I use:

  • Google Search Console, to see which searches the site appears for
  • Bing Webmaster Tools, for how the site shows up in Bing
  • Clarity, to see how people and AI bots actually interact with the pages
  • GA4, for traffic and engagement

I compared three of these in detail in Clarity vs GA4 vs Bing Webmaster Tools.

Making the Site Readable for AI

On the technical side I worked through the basics that help both search engines and AI systems understand a site: a sitemap, robots.txt, IndexNow, an llms.txt file and structured data. I wrote the full list up in getting a website ready for AI search.

Building an AI Team to Do the Work

The part I am most proud of is how the work gets done. I am using my analytics and SEO knowledge to build a team of AI automations, each with its own job, working towards the site's goals. I have read the experts and friends I know in the field, and I am trying to do with an AI support group what they achieved with a whole team.

The way I work with it is simple. I create content, then I ask the system, and Claude, what the data means and how we make it better, and I keep asking until the answer is something I can act on.

Why I Am Doing It This Way

Part of this is a principle. I want to keep everything as low cost as possible, ideally free, to show that anyone can do what I do. A small business owner or a marketer starting out does not need an agency or expensive software to get their site ready for AI search, they need the free tools, some patience and a willingness to keep asking questions. I am hoping that growth mindset keeps this project moving in the right direction.

What I Am Seeing So Far

I am noticing growth. It is not a lot yet, but each week is better than the last, and that makes me really happy. I have my goals and targets, and when we hit them I will set new ones. Until then I will keep using Clarity and GA4 to understand where the opportunities are.

The honest lesson is that AI search does not reward a few pages. It rewards a site that covers its subject properly, so the next step for me is building out much more content around the things I actually know.

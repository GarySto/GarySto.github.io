The Speed of Change in Search Driven by AI.

22 September 2026 · Gary Stow, performance marketing and AI

The speed of change in the search industry driven by AI refers to how quickly new technologies and business models are adopted and integrated into search engines and related services. This pace is visible in three main areas: advertising, pricing, and partnership ecosystems.

AI-powered ads in chat platforms have begun to surface alongside answers. OpenAI started testing ads in ChatGPT on 9 February 2026 for logged-in adult US users on the Free and Go tiers, placing sponsored messages below responses and labelling them as such. The test required a minimum commitment of $200 000 and delivered an approximate CPM of $60 during its initial roll-out. OpenAI begins testing ads in ChatGPT

The pricing model for AI services is also evolving rapidly. A recent review on 6 September 2026 lists Free, Go, Plus and Pro tiers. The Go tier costs $8 a month and includes ads; the higher tiers are ad-free and scale with usage. ChatGPT pricing plans explained

Partnerships between financial platforms and AI providers illustrate how quickly new revenue streams can be created. Revolut announced a collaboration that bundles ChatGPT Go into its subscription plans, offering different access periods for each tier. The same partnership was mirrored by the London Stock Exchange Group, signalling industry-wide acceptance of AI as an add-on service. Revolut opens free ChatGPT Go

In addition to consumer-facing services, banks are embedding AI assistants into their apps. Lloyds Banking Group unveiled a large-scale, multi-feature AI financial assistant built on its own generative and agentic framework, targeting the mobile app in early 2026. The initiative aims to provide customers with conversational tools for portfolio summaries, market data and order placement, all while requiring human approval before execution. Lloyds unveils UK’s first AI financial assistant

Google is also accelerating its AI integration in advertising. At Google Marketing Live 2026, the company announced new Gemini-built ad formats for AI Mode, including Conversational Discovery Ads and Highlighted Answers that appear within recommendation lists. These formats integrate naturally into search results, giving advertisers a direct path to consumers. Google marketing live: new AI-powered ads

The cumulative effect of these developments is a market where the line between content, commerce and conversation is blurring. Advertisers can now target users within an AI assistant’s response; consumers receive personalised answers that may include paid offers; and platforms earn revenue from both ad placements and subscription fees. The speed at which these models are being rolled out suggests that search will continue to evolve beyond keyword matching into a more conversational, AI-driven ecosystem.

For those looking to keep pace, the key is to understand how AI changes the signals that drive relevance. Traditional optimisation techniques still matter, but they must be combined with an awareness of how language models interpret intent and context. Experimentation, data-driven testing and continuous learning are now as essential as creative copy or bid management.

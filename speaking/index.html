Speaking & teaching

Talks and training that leave you with something real.

Alongside the work on the previous page, I spend a good chunk of my own time in front of people talking about AI, and a good chunk more teaching them to actually build with it. If you're planning a panel, a talk, a workshop or a team day, here's the kind of thing I do.

01

Speaking

I've spoken alongside colleagues on where Microsoft AI is heading, and been invited to demo the agents and tools I've built to rooms of industry peers. I'm not chasing a speaker circuit, but I'll happily take a panel, a talk or a fireside chat where I can speak honestly about practical AI adoption rather than reciting a deck.

Panels & fireside chats

Industry conversations

Honest, practitioner-level discussion on AI adoption in performance marketing, not vendor pitching. Comfortable in front of an audience of peers or clients.

Live demo, not slides

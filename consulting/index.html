How I work

Learn first

I change nothing at the start

I learn your systems, your goals and the rhythm of the business first, the same way I start any new role.

Your goal is the measure

Success is set by you

I use your goal, your targets and your constraints as the measure of success, then judge every use of AI against them.

Start small

Working beats impressive

Get the basics working first and skip the bells and whistles. Often a scheduled automation or a good prompt in a tool you already have will do the job.

People decide

Your people keep the final click

AI is a tool or an advisor. The responsibility and the decision stay with your people, and somebody other than the AI checks its work.

This is independent work, done in my own time and separate from my employer. The views here are my own. There is no price list because every team is different.

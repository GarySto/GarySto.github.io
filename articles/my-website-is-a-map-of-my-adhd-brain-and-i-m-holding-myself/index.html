My Website Is a Map of My ADHD Brain, and I'm Holding Myself to Account

31 October 2026 · Gary Stow, performance marketing and AI

Written by Gary Stow with help from AI tools. Every fact is checked and every article is approved by Gary before it goes live.

This website is essentially a view of my ADHD and autistic brain. What started as a simple idea has spiralled, grown and expanded far beyond what I first intended, and this piece is me getting my thoughts out and holding myself to account for that.

I wanted the site to be somewhere to demonstrate my work and what I can do, and to show my growth and development along the way. That was the whole plan.

How it spiralled

Then I started offering guides, and then I decided I wanted to help everyone, so I just gave things away for free. More recently I decided I wanted to make somewhere safe for my daughters, so I started building computer games. All in all, things have expanded a lot.

Where it actually stands

I can be honest and say I'm still not number one in search engines for the key terms I originally wanted to target. According to Microsoft Clarity, I haven't been cited by AI yet either. I am seeing backlinks from people using my GitHub work for their own sites, so there is that, I suppose.

I also managed to get myself banned on Reddit while trying to share some of my thought-piece articles, and I'm learning from that.

Getting back to the point

The site had a pure intention, and it has slowly become a mishmash of everything in my brain. What I'm trying to do now is get it back to its goals and that original intention, without losing anything I've built along the way.

If there's enough interest in any one of these subjects, I might consider spinning up a new website focused only on that. Even with the AI support team I've built for this site, it's hard enough staying on top of everything as it is, so for now the job is focus, and this is me writing it down so I stick to it.

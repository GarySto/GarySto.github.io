Privacy

Privacy.

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This is a personal website run by Gary Stow in the United Kingdom. It has no accounts, no sign-up forms and no shop. The only information collected is basic, anonymous information about how the site is used, so I can see which pages are useful and fix the ones that aren't.

What is collected, and by whom

Google Analytics 4 records things like which pages were visited, roughly where visitors are (country or city level), the device and browser type, and how people arrived at the site. It uses cookies to do this.

Microsoft Clarity records how people use the pages, including clicks, scrolling and anonymised session recordings, so I can spot layout problems. It also uses cookies. Clarity masks text you type.

Microsoft Advertising (UET) measures whether visits came from Microsoft Advertising campaigns.

GitHub Pages and Cloudflare host and deliver the site, and like any web host they process technical information such as your IP address to serve pages and protect the site from abuse.

I don't sell this information, and I don't use it to identify you personally.

If you email me

If you email me, I'll use your email address and message only to reply and to carry on the conversation you started. I won't add you to a mailing list.

Gary HQ reporting tools

I run small reporting tools on my own computer that read this website's own Google Search Console and Google Analytics reports, using read-only access that I've granted to myself. They are used only to understand how this website is performing. They don't access anyone else's Google account or data, and nothing they read is shared with anyone.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site works normally without them.

You can ask what information relates to you, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing me. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Why I built it

My AI teams run my website and my demo trading system every day, but a dashboard full of tables meant nothing to my wife or my children when they asked what I was building. So I drew the whole system as a village. The visual approach made it far easier for them to understand, and it turned out to be the clearest way I have found to explain AI agents to anyone.

What you are looking at

  • Two business districts: the website teams on one side, the trading teams on the other
  • Every page on this site is a house, and more visits mean a bigger house
  • The three demo trading strategies are fields that grow or wilt with their results
  • Anything that needs my decision waits outside the Town Hall
  • Wade, my local AI assistant, thinks for the teams from his tower
  • Builders are always working on the next page

Built to be shared safely

The public village is a separate copy with private details removed and money rounded. It refreshes every evening, and only goes live after an automatic layout check and SEO checklist both pass.

The trading fields show practice-account results, not real income.

A working agent that scores an organisation's AI maturity and produces a structured assessment, built with full handoff documentation so someone else could rebuild or extend it without me.

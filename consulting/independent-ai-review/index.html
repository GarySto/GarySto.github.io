Consulting · Independent AI review

An independent AI review for your marketing team.

I sit with your team for a few weeks, look at how AI is really being used in the day-to-day work, and report back with a plan. It is built for digital marketing teams, in-house or agency, whether that is PPC, paid social, SEO, content or analytics, who have started using AI and want an honest outside view of whether it is working.

01

Why get a review

In my view companies get AI wrong when they treat it as a fix for everything without defining the problem or the cost. Some AI builds cost more in tokens than the person they were meant to replace, when a scheduled automation or a good prompt in a tool the team already has would have done the job.

AI also gets things wrong. I've seen it make things up confidently, ignore instructions and round data until it was inaccurate, which is why I ask for an independent audit of every output I rely on. A team needs the same thing, with somebody outside the work checking it.

I'm an AI consultant who has done the performance marketing job for over ten years, so I know what the work looks like before AI is added to it.

02

What I look at

Time

Where the time really goes

Many teams jump to advanced integrations before looking at the wasted time in their current work. That is where step one should be, so that is where I start.

Prompts

Prompts and context

Most of the time a poor result comes down to whether the prompt was clear and had enough context, knowledge and data, or whether the AI had to guess. I look at how your team asks.

Checking

Who checks the AI

AI shouldn't check its own work. I look at who checks each output, what they check it against, and what happens when it's wrong.

Hand-offs

The joins between people and tools

When I reviewed my own agents, each one looked busy but the work wasn't reaching the next. Teams have the same gaps between people, tools and automations.

Data

What goes into which tool

Free models and work models have different levels of security, and I've seen people put details into an agent without realising. I look at what is being shared and where.

Value

Cost against value

For each use of AI I ask what it's for and whether it saves time or makes money. If it does neither, it goes on the stop list.

03

How it works

  1. We talk first

    You tell me what the team is trying to achieve and what the constraints are, such as time, money, people, knowledge and legal. Those become the measure for everything that follows.

  2. A few weeks with your team

    I sit with the people doing the work and watch how AI is really used. I change nothing at this stage. I learn the systems, the goals and the rhythm first.

  3. The report

    You get a written report in plain English, with an executive summary, the findings with the evidence behind each one, and an action plan in priority order.

  4. We go through it together

    Reading the report and pushing back on it is a separate conversation, and it's one of the most useful parts.

04

What you get

  • What to keep. The uses of AI that are working, written up as examples the rest of the team can copy.
  • What to stop. The ones that cost more time or money than they save.
  • What to do first. A short plan in priority order, starting with the time being wasted today.
  • What to check. The checks to put in place so AI's work is always looked at by somebody else.

The review is independent. I'm not there to sell you a tool, and it isn't legal or compliance advice. This is independent work, done in my own time and separate from my employer. The views here are my own. There is no price list because every team is different.

05

Questions people ask

There is no price list, because a team of three and a department of thirty are different jobs. Tell me about the team and what you want to get out of it, and I will come back with what I would suggest.

Which AI tools do you cover?

I use Copilot, Claude and a local AI model I built at home, and I have built agents in M365 Agent Builder and Copilot Studio. Tell me which tools your team uses, because that changes what I look at.

Is it only for PPC teams?

No. It is for any digital marketing team. I have spent over ten years in performance marketing, and at Ocean Holidays I led a team across PPC, paid social, SEO, affiliates, display and YouTube, and data and analytics.
